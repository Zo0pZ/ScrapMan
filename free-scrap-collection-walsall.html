Free scrap metal collection in Walsall

From Bloxwich to Aldridge, old appliances and offcuts don't need a trip to the household waste centre — collectors come to you.

If it's metal — appliances, radiators, pipes, gates, tools, batteries — you shouldn't have to pay to get rid of it, wait weeks for a slot, or haul it anywhere yourself.

What does Walsall Council charge for bulky waste?

Council bulky waste collection fees vary by council and change often. Across the UK, the average minimum charge for a council bulky waste collection is £37.70 (Business Waste survey of published council fees, June 2026) — and you usually wait days or weeks for a collection slot.

For Walsall's current prices and booking, check Walsall Council's website and search for “bulky waste”.

For scrap metal, though, there's usually no need to pay at all: metal has scrap value, so local collectors will pick it up free.
