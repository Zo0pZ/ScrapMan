Bulky waste charges across the West Midlands

Getting a council to take away bulky waste is rarely free. The average minimum charge for a council bulky waste collection across the UK is £37.70, up 17.8% in a year (Business Waste survey of published council fees, June 2026) — and what you'll pay is a postcode lottery, ranging from free in a handful of council areas to over £90 in others.

Here's the thing: if the stuff you're clearing is metal — washing machines, cookers, radiators, gates, pipes, tools — you probably don't need the council at all. Scrap metal has real value, so local collectors will pick it up from your doorstep for free.
